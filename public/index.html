The Desire Discovery Assessment

A structured self-assessment of sexual interests across 50 categories, your desire patterns, and your pattern of attraction.

  • Coverage. 50 categories from mainstream to niche, including which side of each two-sided practice applies to you.
  • Method. Six sections: agreement statements that score your profile dimensions, weighted scenarios, a direct interest inventory with calibration follow-ups, desire and solo patterns, open-response items in your own words, and attraction items. About ten minutes; there are no right or wrong answers.
  • Results. A summary of your strongest signals on screen when you finish. Your full report, with a percentage and explanation for every category, arrives by email.
  • Delivery. Your full report is sent directly to the email address you provide. Your data is handled securely.

Adults 18+. All practices referenced assume consent between adults.
